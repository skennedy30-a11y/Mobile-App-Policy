## Privacy Policy — My Drum Tab

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

My Drum Tab ("the app") is designed to protect your privacy. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

### We do not collect your data

My Drum Tab does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information. Specifically:

- **No account is required.** The app has no sign-in, login, or user profile.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app does not use third-party analytics,
  advertising, or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps
  or websites.
- **No data leaves your device.** Your drum charts, song charts, settings, and
  any content you create are stored **only on your device**. They are not
  uploaded to us or to any server.

### Content you create

Charts and song charts you create are saved locally on your iPad. When you
choose to **export or share** a chart (for example as a PDF, PNG, MIDI, text,
or chart file), that file is handled by the standard iOS share sheet and goes
only to the destination you pick (such as Files, Mail, or another app). We are
not involved in and do not receive that content.

### Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under
the age of 13.

### Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or
data-collection services.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
"Last updated" date.
