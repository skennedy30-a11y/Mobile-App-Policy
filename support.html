Common questions

  • Where are my charts saved? On your device. Charts are stored locally and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • How do I back up or move a chart? Use Export to save a chart file (or PDF/MIDI), then re-import it on another device.
  • Which devices are supported? My Drum Tab is designed for iPad (iPadOS 17 and later).
